HuhnLite 

Here you can find out how this program came about.
It began in 1988, when the first version of the MSDOS program was released.HUHN was released. The last update was in 1992 as version 3.1.
HUHN was a multi-user version – with a text interface, Btrieve/N as the file system, and written in Turbo Pascal. It was developed in collaboration with Riehl & Partner GmbH, who also handled distribution.

So it was time to develop a new application adapted to technological progress.
HuhnLite is ideal for small to medium-sized laying hen farms.HuhnLite It is designed as a single-user version. The limitation relates to the database used.Sqlite.It is not designed for competing work. However, it can be performed from multiple workstations, but not simultaneously.

5. Daily recordings & bookings (central office)

Welcome to the operational core ofHuhnLiteThis module is your daily digital stable management board. Instead of clicking through endless submenus, this central hub brings together all the tasks that arise during day-to-day operations in one place.

You can easily switch between the individual data entry forms using the tabs at the top.

💡 Practical tip for a quick start:The system is so intelligent that it can remember where you came from. For example, if you scan a sign with a QR code on the farm, the application will automatically open directly to the appropriate tab (e.g., immediately to silo recording or barn log), without you having to search for it manually!

🗂️ Overview: The seven everyday building blocks

Depending on the task you are currently performing, select the appropriate tab:

1. 🥚 Performance (performance measurement per herd)

The most important tool for every day. Here you record how many eggs your animals have laid.

  • What is being recorded here?The daily number of eggs, sorted according to your desired quality and weight classes (e.g. class A, cracked or broken eggs) as well as the data for regular weight checks of your hens.

2. 🪶 Losses (Quick entry for animal deaths)

If animals die in the barn, this must be recorded immediately for documentation purposes and to accurately calculate the current animal population.

  • What is being recorded here?The number of dead or euthanized hens, including a quick selection of the cause (e.g., disease, age, or bird of prey).

3. 🐓 Animal movements (population changes)

You use this module for the major events in the life cycle of a herd that are not part of the daily loss-making business.

  • What is being recorded here?The first timeInstallnew young hense Moving the stablesof animals to other barn sections as well as the final sale or disposal of the old hens at the end of the laying period (expulsion).

4. 📦 Egg storage (warehouse management & sorting)

Your digital inventory book. Here you control the flow of eggs after they have left the henhouse.

  • What is being recorded here?The transfer of egg pallets between different storage areas (e.g. from the sorting room to the main cold storage) as well as manual inventory adjustments.

5. 🌾 Feed (Silo Management & Deliveries)

To ensure accurate feed balance and cost control, the receipt of feed goods is documented here.

  • What is being recorded here?Each new feed delivery from your dealer, broken down into the respective feed silos, including delivery note numbers, prices and qualities.

6. 💰 Sales (Direct Marketing & Trade)

This section details the economic aspects of egg production.

  • What is being recorded here?The commercial documentation of egg sales to large customers or the farm shop – including the assignment of batch numbers for complete traceability.

7. 📅 Actions (Tasks, vaccinations & notes)

The digital memory for herd management. Here you plan and document everything that goes beyond the raw numbers.

  • What is being recorded here?Upcoming or completed mandatory vaccinations, official inspection appointments, veterinary findings, or simple to-do lists for your staff in the barn.

13. Background Tables & System Configuration

This module is the logical foundation of HuhnLite. It does not handle everyday barn bookings, but rather maintains the central guidelines, price lists, and tax rates that the program needs for its automatic calculations in the background.

The administration is clearly divided into six departments (tabs):

📅 Register 1: Age tables (breeders' reference values)

Here you enter the official performance data from the breeding companies for your chicken breeds. The system will later use these values ​​to compare the actual performance of your flock in the coop with the breeders' optimal specifications.

a) Table header data (The template)

  • Table number & name:A unique number and a name (e.g.“Lohmann Brown 2026 Guidelines”).

  • Date of creation & validity:Documents when this table may be used for comparisons.

b) Row data (The weekly specifications)

For each individual week of life The following guidelines are provided for the hens:

  • Legerate AH / DH (%):The expected daily egg yield is based on the number of hens housed (initial helpers) or the current stock (average helpers).

  • Egg weight per week / Cumulative (g):  The average weight of an egg this week, as well as the overall average since laying began.

  • Egg mass & eggs cumulative: The projected total egg mass in grams and the total number of eggs a hen should have laid on average by this week of her life.

⚖️ Register 2: Weight tables (size sorting)

This table controls one of the program's cleverest functions: it determines how the eggs are statistically distributed across the weight classes for a given average weight.

  • The sorting logic:You enter an average weight in grams (g). The system now requires a percentage breakdown, which in total...exactly 100%must result (divided among the classes)XL, L1, L2, M1, M2, S1, S2).

  • The practical advantage:If you weigh a sample in the barn and determine the average weight, the program can immediately use this table to mathematically estimate how many XL, L, or M eggs are on the pallets today, without you having to sort each egg individually!

📊 Value Added Tax (Tax Office Settings)

Here you maintain the tax framework for your commercial bookings, which are needed, for example, when purchasing feed.

  • Tax identification number (MWSTKZ): A short abbreviation for the tax rate (e.g.„A“for the full set,„B“for the reduced rate).

  • Percentage (%)PERCENT): The current tax rate (e.g. 19.0% or 7.0%).

  • Account (ACCOUNT): An optional field for the account number of your financial accounting system (FiBu), in case you wish to transfer the data to a tax office later.

💰 Register 4: Price tables (Central egg prices)

Automatic price memory for your egg sales. When you initiate a sale in the egg storage area, the system consults this table to accurately display the prices.

  • Posture type (Concentration camp attitude type): Determines the housing method (e.g.Organic, free-range or floor housing).

  • Egg classes (EGG CLASSES): The weight class for which the price applies (e.g.XL, L, M, S).

  • Weight from/to (g):The weight range in grams for this class.

  • Price range (€):The default selling price.

🌾 Register 5: Types of feed (feed catalog)

A very simple, clean list of all feedstuffs used on your farm.

  • Designation (DESIGNATION): The name of the feed (e.g.“Organic laying hen meal Phase 1” or „Junghennenfutter“ The varieties listed here are immediately available to your employees as a clean selection menu in the feed receiving area.

⚙️ Register 6: Field Configuration (The Translation Center)

Note: This is a deep system function that should generally only be changed by the administrator or system manager.

This table translates the "technical language" of the database into understandable terms for the user interface.

  • Technical name (FELDNAME): The exact name of the column in the database (e.g.INITIAL STOCK).

  • Display name / translation (CONTENTS): The word that the user sees on the screen (e.g.“Initial animal population”).

  • Short title / Subject (REFERENCE): Additional text or a short description for column headers and tables.

  • Available in database (NAMEINDB): A yes/no switch (flag). It indicates to the system whether it is a real, physical field in the database or a value calculated live by the program (a so-called "alias").

🔄 The automatic process during a recovery:

Since this process irrevocably overwrites all current data, the system has a built-in safety net:

Confirmation:You must explicitly confirm the process in a warning window.

Automatic security backup: Before the program imports the old data, the backend creates A fully automatic, secret backup copy (safety copy) is created. of the current status. So if you made a mistake in the line, nothing is lost.

Automatic restart: Once the data has been imported, the app forces a complete restart of the application This completely clears the internal memory of the Vue app and loads it fresh with the recovered data.
